How to Enroll

1. Enrollment Steps

  1. Choose Your Program: Select between Bachelor of Elementary Education (BEED) or Bachelor of Secondary Education (BSED).
  2. Prepare Your Requirements: Gather the required documents based on your student category.
  3. Submit Documents: Visit the Southland College Registrar’s Office on campus (Rizal St., Kabankalan City) to submit your documents and complete the registration form.
  4. Assessment & Official Enrollment: Receive your official enrollment assessment to finalize your registration.

2. Document Checklist by Category

  • New / Freshmen & Returning Students:
    • Accomplished Registration and Admissions Form
    • School Form 9 (Report Card / Form 138)
    • Photocopy of Live Birth Certificate (PSA / NSO)
  • Transferees:
    • Accomplished Registration and Admissions Form
    • Official Transcript of Records (TOR)
    • Certificate of Transfer Credential / Honorable Dismissal
    • Photocopy of Live Birth Certificate (PSA / NSO)
    • Advice Slip from the STED Dean / Program Head
  • Continuing Students:
    • Accomplished Registration and Admissions Form
    • Prospectus with Evaluated Grades
