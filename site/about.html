History

Foundation of Southland College (2009)

Southland College was founded on March 24, 2009, in Kabankalan City as a private, non-sectarian institution designed to deliver high-quality tertiary education to students in Southern Negros Occidental.

Vision

The zenith educational institution that produces top-tier graduates to reign supreme in the global aren

Mission

Southland College is committed to enlightening the enterprising minds and molding the hearts of individuals as beacons of success in all aspects of their life and career in the midst of the metamorphic world

S – Service

Dedication to serving God, the institution, the community, and the country with humility and selflessness.

Core Values

L – Leadership

Inspiring and empowering others through vision, sound judgment, character, and positive influence.

E – Excellence

Striving for the highest quality and standards in academic performance, leadership, and professional practice.

S – Synergy

Fostering teamwork, collaboration, and mutual respect to achieve shared goals and holistic growth.

A – Accountability

Taking full responsibility and ownership for one's actions, decisions, and ethical conduct.
