STED Programs

Bachelor of Elementary Education (BEED)

equips future educators with the holistic pedagogical knowledge, multi-subject expertise, and classroom experience needed to inspire and guide young learners across all primary grade levels.

Focus Areas

Child DevelopmentInclusive EducationMulti-Disciplinary Content Pedagogy

Bachelor of Secondary Education Major in Filipino (BSEd Filipino)

prepares future secondary educators with deep expertise in the Filipino language, rich literary traditions, and modern teaching methodologies to effectively instruct high school students.

Focus Areas

BalarilaMetodolohiya

Bachelor of Secondary Education Major in English (BSEd English)

prepares prospective secondary educators with advanced language proficiency, a deep appreciation of global literature, and modern pedagogical skills to teach English effectively in high school settings.

Focus Areas

English LinguisticsGrammarLiterary Criticism

Bachelor of Secondary Education Major in Mathematics (BSEd Mathematics)

trains future secondary educators with strong analytical skills, deep mathematical knowledge, and innovative teaching techniques to make complex math concepts accessible and engaging for high school students.

Focus Areas

Advanced MathematicsMathematical ReasoningProblem Solving

Bachelor of Secondary Education Major in Social Science (BSEd SS)

equips future secondary educators with a comprehensive understanding of human society, history, and civic institutions, along with the pedagogical tools needed to foster critical thinking and active citizenship in high school students.

Focus Areas

Philippine & World HistoryGeographyEconomics & Governance
